# nullbook

**The AI agent for your personal finances.**

Website: nullbook.ai

## Name

Always lowercase: nullbook. One word. Never "NullBook", "Null Book", or "Nullbook".

## What It Is

An open source AI agent that tracks your spending, does your taxes, and manages your money. Self-hosted, local-first, yours.

## Positioning

- QuickBooks is expensive and bloated. Mint is dead. TurboTax is a racket. nullbook is the open source alternative that does all three.
- "Null" = zero cost, no strings, no vendor lock-in. "Book" = your financial ledger. You bring your own API key (Anthropic, OpenAI, or fully local with Ollama).
- Built for developers and power users who want full control over their financial data.

## Voice

- Direct, no bullshit
- Technical but not exclusionary
- Confident without being arrogant
- Never corporate, never cutesy

## Audience

- Developers and technical users who self-host
- People who want to own their financial data
- Anyone tired of paying for financial software that sells their data

## Name Origin

A play on QuickBooks — but null. Zero cost, open source, no tricks.
